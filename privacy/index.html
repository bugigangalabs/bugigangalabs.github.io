Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

This policy covers the games published by Bugiganga Labs ("we"):

  • 3Diffind, on Android, iOS and the web

In short: our games do not collect, store or share any personal information. They have no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and they send nothing over the internet.

What the games keep on your device

A game saves your progress (the puzzles you have played, the differences you have found and the clues you have) and your settings (such as sound volume) on your device. This information stays there: we never receive it.

To delete it, uninstall the game, or reset your progress in the game's settings.

Backups

If you have turned on your device's own backup (Google's on Android, iCloud on iPhone and iPad), it may include the game's save, so that your progress comes back on a new device. That backup is kept in your account by Google or Apple, under their privacy policies. We cannot see it.

Stores and websites

You get our games through Google Play, the App Store or itch.io. Those services may collect information when you download or play, under their own privacy policies, and may share anonymous, combined statistics with us, such as the number of installs or crash reports.

  • Apple privacy policy

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13. They collect no information from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If a game ever starts collecting information, for example to show ads or to offer purchases, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date at the top of this page.
